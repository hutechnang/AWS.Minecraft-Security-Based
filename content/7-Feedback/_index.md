## General Evaluation

### 1. Working Environment

The learning and working environment at the **First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ) Bootcamp** is highly professional and quiet, which helped me study more effectively and progress faster. The mentors, trainers, and fellow members are always ready to support whenever difficulties arise, creating favorable conditions for every trainee to exchange knowledge and develop skills. The working and learning spaces are well-organized, offering comfort and enabling full focus on the research and project development processes.

---

### 2. Support from Mentors and the Organizing Committee

Throughout the internship, the mentors provided dedicated guidance on any questions, resolved technical issues, and offered suitable directions during project execution. Instead of merely providing direct answers, mentors often encouraged trainees to proactively research, experiment, and find solutions on their own before giving necessary feedback.

Additionally, the Organizing Committee and support team always facilitated easy access to documentation, infrastructure, and learning activities, ensuring the internship progressed efficiently and on schedule.

---

### 3. Relevance Between Internship Content and Major

The internship content aligns closely with the Information Technology major, particularly in the fields of Cloud Computing, Cyber Security, and DevOps.

Through researching and deploying the **"Minecraft Server Security Platform on AWS"** project, I had the opportunity to apply my knowledge of computer networks, operating systems, information security, and programming. Concurrently, I gained exposure to many modern AWS services, including Amazon GuardDuty, AWS Lambda, Amazon SNS, AWS Backup, Systems Manager Session Manager, and Global Accelerator.

This not only helped me consolidate my core academic foundation but also deepened my understanding of how cloud services are utilized in real-world systems.

---

### 4. Opportunities for Learning and Skill Development

The Bootcamp brings numerous opportunities for personal growth and learning through activities such as:

- Participating in building real-world projects on the AWS platform.
- Learning how to design system architectures according to practical industry standards.
- Improving skills in researching technical documentation and constructing project documentation.
- Practicing teamwork, time management, and presenting ideas before the community.

These are valuable experiences that help better prepare me for a professional working environment after graduation.

---

### 5. Culture and Team Spirit

One of the aspects I appreciate the most at FCAJ is the spirit of sharing and mutual support among members.

The mentors and trainees are always ready to exchange knowledge and support each other during research and project development. Everyone works with a high sense of responsibility and mutual respect, constantly encouraging learning from mistakes rather than just focusing on the final outcomes.

This made me feel like an integral part of the community, motivating me to actively learn and contribute more to the team.

---

### 6. Training Program and Intern Benefits

The Bootcamp delivers valuable training content, ranging from fundamental AWS knowledge to hands-on system deployment skills. This has helped me develop both my technical expertise and essential soft skills.

---

## Other Questions

### What I Was Most Satisfied With During the Internship

What satisfied me the most was being immersed in a practical learning environment where I had the chance to research, deploy, and complete a Cloud Security project from the ideation stage to full implementation.

---

### Am I Willing to Recommend This Program to Friends?

**Yes.**

I am completely willing to recommend the First Cloud AI Journey Bootcamp to my friends. It is a highly practical program that not only gives learners exposure to AWS knowledge but also opens up opportunities to participate in real-world projects, technical workshops, and build a strong network within the Cloud Computing community.

This is the ideal environment for anyone aspiring to develop their career in Cloud, DevOps, AI, or Cyber Security.

---

## Suggestions and Expectations

In the future, I hope the program can support team members in registering to work at the office on the same days so that we can collaborate and discuss tasks more easily.

---

### Additional Comments

I would like to express my sincere gratitude to the Organizing Committee, the mentors, and all members of the First Cloud AI Journey Bootcamp for creating a professional, open, and highly inspiring learning environment.

The knowledge, experience, and insights I acquired throughout this internship will serve as a crucial foundation helping me continue to perfect myself and pursue my career path in the field of Cloud Computing and Cyber Security.
